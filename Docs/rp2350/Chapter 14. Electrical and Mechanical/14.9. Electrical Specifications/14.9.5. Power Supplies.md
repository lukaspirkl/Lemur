# **NOTE**

RP2354 contains an internal 3.3V flash device, therefore QSPI_IOVDD *must* be 3.3V. Furthermore, if the QSPI pins are to be used to connect to an additional flash or PSRAM device, then IOVDD *must* be 3.3V, as a GPIO is used as QSPI chip select in this case.
