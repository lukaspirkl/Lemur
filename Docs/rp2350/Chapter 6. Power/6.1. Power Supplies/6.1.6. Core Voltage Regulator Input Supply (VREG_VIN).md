#### <span id="page-440-2"></span>**6.1.6. Core Voltage Regulator Input Supply (VREG_VIN)**

VREG_VIN is the input supply for the on-chip core voltage regulator, and should be in the range 2.7 V to 5.5 V. To reduce the number of external power supplies, VREG_VIN can use the same power source as the voltage regulator analogue supply (VREG_AVDD), or digital IO supply (IOVDD). Though care should be taken to minimise the noise on VREG_AVDD.

A 4.7μF capacitor should be connected between VREG_VIN and ground close to the chip's VREG_VIN pin.

For more details on the on-chip voltage regulator see [Section 6.3](#page-446-0).
