# <span id="page-448-2"></span>**6.3.6. Over Temperature Protection**

The voltage regulator will terminate regulation, and disable its power transistors, if the transistor junction temperature rises above a threshold set by the HT_TH field in the [VREG_CTRL](#page-460-0) register. The regulator will restart regulation when the transistor junction temperature drops to approximately 20°C below the temperature threshold.
